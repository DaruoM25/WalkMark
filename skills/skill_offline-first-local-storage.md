### LOCAL-FIRST RULES
1. WalkMark operates 100% offline without mandatory user accounts or cloud login.
2. Attached photos are stored in app-private sandbox storage and referenced by local URI/path.
3. Notes and GPS coordinates persist exclusively on the local device.
