# Privacy & Data Sovereignty - [PLANNED]

- **Zero-tracking policy**: No ads, no analytics trackers, no social networking SDKs.
- **Local-first**: GPS routes, notes, and photos reside exclusively on your physical device.
- **Support is user initiated**: Opening Help & Support collects and sends nothing. Contact support opens the configured email application only after an explicit action and never automatically attaches routes, locations, notes, photos, logs, identifiers, or database contents.
